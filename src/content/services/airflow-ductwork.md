## Symptoms of airflow trouble

These are the calls we get constantly — and they're rarely "broken equipment" problems:

- One room or floor that never cools down
- Weak airflow at the registers even though the unit runs
- Humidity that never settles, or the house feels clammy
- Temperature swings between rooms
- System runs forever but comfort never improves
- Noisy ducts, whistling registers, or doors that slam themselves shut

## What we actually check

Airflow work is measurement work. Guessing at duct problems gets expensive fast, so we measure:

- **Static pressure and total system airflow** — is the blower moving the air it should?
- **Supply and return balance** — is air getting where it needs to go, and can it get back?
- **Duct condition** — leakage, kinks, crushed or disconnected runs, undersized transitions
- **Filter and coil condition** — restrictions that quietly strangle airflow
- **Return air paths** — closed doors and missing returns starve rooms of circulation
- **Fan settings and equipment matchup** — the blower doing its part for your ducts

Static pressure helps separate airflow restrictions from problems on the refrigeration side.
Combined with measured airflow and refrigerant readings, it shows whether the fix is in the
ducts, the blower, or the equipment. When the readings point to the refrigerant side, that's
covered under [AC repair & diagnostics](/services/ac-repair-diagnostics/); the same measurement
discipline carries into [TAB and commissioning support](/tab-commissioning-support/) for
professional teams.

## What fixes look like

Depends entirely on what we find — that's the point of measuring first:

- Sealing or repairing leaking duct runs
- Adding or enlarging returns so rooms can actually breathe
- Balancing supply air between rooms
- Correcting fan speed or equipment settings
- Practical retrofits for problem rooms, quoted before any work starts
