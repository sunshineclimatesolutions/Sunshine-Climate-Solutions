**Premium AC Maintenance. Comprehensive Care. Just $75.**

This is preventive maintenance for a system that is running: routine cleaning, testing and a
real evaluation of how your equipment is operating. **$75 per system, per visit** — an
individual maintenance visit, not a membership, annual contract, repair warranty or
subscription. If your system isn't cooling or isn't running, start with a
[repair diagnostic](/services/ac-repair-diagnostics/) instead.

## The four cleaning services included in every visit

These routine procedures are part of the advertised $75 price — not add-ons:

- **Outdoor condenser-coil cleaning.**
- **Routine cleaning of the accessible indoor evaporator coil.**
- **Flushing of the accessible condensate drain.**
- **Accessible blower-compartment cleaning.**

Every visit also includes the electrical inspection and operating evaluation described below.

## The full maintenance visit

### System inspection

- Inspect accessible indoor and outdoor equipment.
- Check thermostat settings and cooling operation.
- Verify normal cooling startup and shutdown.
- Inspect accessible refrigerant piping and insulation.
- Check accessible equipment panels and visible connections.
- Investigate unusual noise, vibration or visible deterioration.
- Evaluate general equipment operation.

### Electrical inspection and testing

- Measure applicable supply and operating voltages.
- Test applicable run and start capacitors.
- Inspect applicable contactors and relays.
- Measure compressor operating amperage where appropriate.
- Evaluate indoor and outdoor fan-motor operation and applicable amperage.
- Inspect accessible electrical connections and wiring.
- Check applicable controls and accessible safety devices.

Procedures are matched to your equipment. Inverter systems and other modern equipment may not
contain conventional capacitors or contactors — testing follows manufacturer-appropriate
methods.

### Cleaning and drainage

- Outdoor condenser-coil cleaning.
- Routine cleaning of the accessible indoor evaporator coil.
- Flushing of the accessible condensate drain.
- Accessible blower-compartment cleaning.
- Check the condensate pan and accessible drain connections.
- Check the outdoor unit for airflow obstructions.
- Verify accessible condensate drainage after flushing.
- Check installed condensate overflow protection where applicable.

Cleaning follows manufacturer requirements and equipment-safe procedures.

### Airflow and performance

- Inspect the installed air filter.
- Check accessible return-air and supply-air restrictions.
- Measure supply-air and return-air temperatures.
- Evaluate the cooling temperature difference under appropriate operating conditions.
- Evaluate blower operation and accessible settings.
- Assess refrigerant-system performance using appropriate operating indicators.
- Measure external static pressure where suitable testing access exists.
- Note obvious accessible ductwork or airflow concerns.

### Final verification

- Recheck system operation after maintenance.
- Document relevant operating measurements.
- Identify notable equipment conditions.
- Explain findings and available next steps.
- Provide a concise maintenance summary.

## What is not included

The $75 visit covers the preventive maintenance above. It does not include, and never
automatically adds:

- Replacement air filters or filter materials (we inspect your filter; replacements are not
  included).
- Refrigerant additions and leak repairs.
- Replacement electrical or mechanical components.
- Invasive or extensive coil cleaning.
- Full blower-wheel removal and deep cleaning.
- Major condensate-system repairs.
- Complete duct-system balancing.
- Additional repairs discovered during maintenance.

If we find something that needs work beyond the visit, we explain it and give you a clear price
first. **Nothing additional proceeds without your authorization.**

## Access and equipment limitations

Some components are not accessible on every system without invasive disassembly, and routine
maintenance must stay within manufacturer-safe procedures. When a procedure in this list cannot
be performed due to normal access limits, we tell you at the visit and note it in the summary —
we don't quietly skip an advertised procedure, and we don't add a surprise charge for ordinary
access.

Two honest boundaries:

- Routine indoor-coil cleaning does not mean coil removal, invasive cabinet reconstruction or
  restoration of severely impacted components.
- Routine blower-compartment cleaning does not automatically include removing the entire blower
  assembly for deep cleaning.

Heating, gas-furnace service and combustion testing are separate services and are not part of
this maintenance visit.

## Maintenance vs. a service call

These are two different visits:

- **Premium AC Maintenance ($75 per system, per visit)** is for a system that's operating —
  routine cleaning, testing and evaluation to keep it in good condition.
- **A repair service call ($50, waived when you proceed with the repair)** is for a system that
  isn't working correctly — diagnostics to find and explain the problem.

If you're not sure which applies, call or text and describe what's happening — we'll tell you
straight.

## When maintenance makes sense

- Your system is running normally and you want it cleaned, tested and evaluated.
- You're heading into the cooling season and want the coils and drain in good condition.
- It's been a long time since the equipment was cleaned or checked.
- You want documented operating measurements and a plain-language summary of your equipment's
  condition.

Maintenance is preventive care. It does not guarantee that equipment will never break down or
restore a specific amount of lost efficiency — it's a professional routine visit that keeps
your system clean, tested and honestly evaluated.

## Maintenance FAQ

### How long does a maintenance visit take?

It depends on the system — outdoor coil condition, indoor access and equipment type all affect
the time needed. We don't rush through cleanings, and we don't leave until the visit items are
done or explained.

### What does "$75 per system, per visit" mean if I have two systems?

The price is per system. Two systems scheduled for the same visit are two maintenance services
— $75 each.

### Is this a subscription or contract?

No. It's an individual visit. There's no membership, no annual contract and no automatic
renewal.

### Do you clean the coils for that price?

Yes — routine outdoor condenser-coil cleaning and routine cleaning of the accessible indoor
evaporator coil are included, along with condensate drain flushing and accessible
blower-compartment cleaning.

### My system needs a repair. Should I schedule maintenance instead?

No. Maintenance is for an operating system. If something is broken or not cooling, schedule a
[repair diagnostic](/services/ac-repair-diagnostics/) — the $50 service call applies, and it's
waived when you proceed with the repair.

### Will you tell me if you find a problem?

Yes. Every visit ends with findings explained in plain language and a concise summary. If we
recommend additional work, you get the reason and the price — and the decision is yours.

### Do you service my area?

We're based in Spring Hill and serve Hernando, Pasco, Pinellas and Hillsborough counties —
[see the full service area](/service-area/) or call to confirm your address.
