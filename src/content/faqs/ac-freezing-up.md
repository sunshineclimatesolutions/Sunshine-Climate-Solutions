---
question: Why does my AC freeze up?
category: repairs
order: 4
---

Ice on the indoor coil almost always means either airflow is restricted (dirty filter, dirty
coil, blower problem, or duct restriction) or refrigerant pressure is low. Running the system
with a frozen coil can send liquid refrigerant back to the compressor, so the cause matters as
much as the thaw. We measure airflow, static pressure, and refrigerant readings, then fix the
cause rather than the symptom. Airflow-side causes are covered under
[airflow & ductwork](/services/airflow-ductwork/).
