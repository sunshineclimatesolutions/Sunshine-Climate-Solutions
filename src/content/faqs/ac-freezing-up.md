---
question: Why does my AC freeze up?
category: repairs
order: 4
---

Ice on the indoor coil usually means one of two things: airflow is restricted (dirty filter, dirty
coil, blower problem, or a duct restriction), or refrigerant pressure is low. Running the system
with a frozen coil can send liquid refrigerant back to the compressor, so the cause matters as
much as the thaw. We measure airflow, static pressure, and refrigerant readings before
recommending anything — airflow-side causes are covered under
[airflow & ductwork](/services/airflow-ductwork/).
