We accept:

- Credit and debit cards (processed through Stripe)
- Bank transfer (ACH)
- Checks
- Cash

Other options — PayPal, Klarna, and cryptocurrency — can be arranged directly with the business. We don't collect payment information through this website, and there's no checkout here: payment is handled with you directly.
