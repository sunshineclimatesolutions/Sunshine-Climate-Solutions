---
question: How does requesting service work?
category: process
order: 1
---

Three ways to start: call (727) 661-5200, text us, or submit the short request form on the [contact page](/contact/).

After you reach out, we contact you to confirm availability — submitting a request does not book an appointment. Once confirmed, we evaluate your system on site, explain what we find in plain language, and no work proceeds until you approve it.
