---
question: What is static pressure, and why does it matter?
category: repairs
order: 6
---

Static pressure is the resistance to airflow inside the duct system. When it's too high, airflow
drops even though the equipment is running: weak registers, rooms that never get comfortable,
humidity problems, and extra strain on the blower motor. It's the fastest way to tell an airflow
problem from a refrigeration problem. More detail on the
[airflow & ductwork page](/services/airflow-ductwork/).
