---
question: What is static pressure, and why does it matter?
category: repairs
order: 6
---

Static pressure is the resistance to airflow inside the duct system. When it's too high,
airflow drops even though the equipment is running — causing weak registers, rooms that never
get comfortable, humidity problems, and extra strain on the blower motor. We measure it because
it's the fastest way to tell an airflow problem apart from a refrigeration problem before
recommending any repair. More detail on the
[airflow & ductwork page](/services/airflow-ductwork/).
