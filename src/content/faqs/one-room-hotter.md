---
question: Why is one room hotter than the rest?
category: repairs
order: 5
---

A room that runs hotter is usually a distribution problem, not an equipment problem: duct sizing
or restrictions, closed doors, missing return paths, or an unbalanced system. Static pressure and
measured airflow show where the air is going — and whether the fix is balancing, a duct
modification, or a restriction elsewhere. See
[airflow & ductwork](/services/airflow-ductwork/).
