question: Why is one room hotter than the rest?
category: repairs
order: 5
---

Room-to-room differences are usually about air distribution rather than the equipment itself:
duct sizing or restrictions, closed doors, missing return-air paths, or an unbalanced system.
Measuring static pressure and actual airflow shows where the air is going — and whether the
fix is balancing, a duct modification, or a restriction elsewhere in the system. See
[airflow & ductwork](/services/airflow-ductwork/) for the diagnostic approach.
