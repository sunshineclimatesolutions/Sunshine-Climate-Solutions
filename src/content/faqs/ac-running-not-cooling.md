question: Why is my AC running but not cooling?
category: repairs
order: 2
---

A system that runs but doesn't cool usually points to one of three things: an airflow
restriction (filter, coil, blower, or ductwork), a refrigerant or mechanical problem, or an
electrical/control fault. The symptoms overlap, so guessing gets expensive — we measure
airflow and static pressure, refrigerant readings, and electrical values at the board and
components, then explain the root cause in plain language. See
[AC repair & diagnostics](/services/ac-repair-diagnostics/) for how a visit works.
