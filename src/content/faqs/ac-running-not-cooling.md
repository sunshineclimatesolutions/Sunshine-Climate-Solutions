---
question: Why is my AC running but not cooling?
category: repairs
order: 2
---

A system that runs without cooling usually points to one of three things: an airflow restriction,
a refrigerant or mechanical problem, or an electrical/control fault. The symptoms overlap, so
guessing gets expensive. We measure airflow and static pressure, refrigerant readings, and
electrical values at the board and components, then show you the cause.
[AC repair & diagnostics](/services/ac-repair-diagnostics/) covers how a visit works.
