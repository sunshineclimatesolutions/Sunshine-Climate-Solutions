---
question: Why does my AC keep tripping the breaker?
category: repairs
order: 3
---

Breakers trip for real reasons: shorted or grounded wiring, failing compressor windings, a failing
fan motor, loose connections, or a weak breaker. Resetting it repeatedly without finding the cause
can damage the compressor. The circuit gets tested at the board, contactor, and motors before any
part is replaced — part of a standard [diagnostic visit](/services/ac-repair-diagnostics/).
