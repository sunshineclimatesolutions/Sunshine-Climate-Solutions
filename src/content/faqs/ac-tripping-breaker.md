---
question: Why does my AC keep tripping the breaker?
category: repairs
order: 3
---

Breakers trip for real electrical reasons: shorted or grounded wiring, failing compressor
windings, a failing fan motor, loose connections, or a weak breaker itself. Resetting the
breaker repeatedly without diagnosing the cause can damage the compressor, so it's worth
testing the circuit properly — electrical values at the board, contactor, and motors — before
replacing parts. That's part of a standard [diagnostic visit](/services/ac-repair-diagnostics/).
