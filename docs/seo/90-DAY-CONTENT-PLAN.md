# 90-day content plan — September 2026

**Authoritative strategic plan** for the first 90 days of the organic marketing program.
This file answers **who each week targets, what the anchor is, which page owns it, which CTA
it carries, where it is distributed, what Field Proof it needs, what website action it
implies, and what to measure**.

- Execution manual (production workflow, SOPs, measurement, decision rules):
  `docs/marketing/CONTENT-OPERATING-SYSTEM.md`
- Week-by-week tracker: `docs/marketing/90-DAY-CONTENT-CALENDAR.csv`
- Weekly KPI capture: `docs/marketing/WEEKLY-MARKETING-SCORECARD.md`
- Review workflow: `docs/marketing/REVIEW-GROWTH-SYSTEM.md`

## Principles (non-negotiable — preserved from the original plan)

- **One owning page per query/service cluster.** Supporting content feeds that page; clusters
  are expanded on the owning page, never duplicated into new pages.
- **No doorway pages.** No city-page farms, no near-duplicate pages, no thin templated posts.
  The Spring Hill hub is the only location page (`LOCAL-PAGE-ROADMAP.md`).
- **No keyword stuffing, no filler content.** No "10 summer tips", no AI dumps, no invented
  lists.
- **No fabricated claims.** No fake reviews, invented project counts, fabricated
  measurements, invented savings, or fabricated availability. Field measurements always
  require context; one measurement is never presented as a universal HVAC standard.
- **Real questions, real expertise.** Every piece starts from a question a homeowner,
  contractor, or property manager actually asks.
- **One primary CTA per content item.** Never stack multiple asks.
- **Field Proof is never staged.** Genuine jobs, genuine measurements, privacy respected.
- **Never attack competitors.** Teach customers how to evaluate HVAC scope intelligently.
- **No unsafe DIY instructions** — electrical or refrigerant.
- **UTM discipline:** posts use the exact prepared links from
  `docs/marketing/UTM-MASTER-LINKS.md`; internal navigation, canonicals and the sitemap stay
  UTM-free; Google Ads remains auto-tagged and separate.

## The two engines

Residential and commercial/TAB audiences share technical credibility but never share
language, CTAs, proof, channels, or landing pages.

| | **Engine A — Residential Demand** | **Engine B — Commercial / TAB Authority** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audience | Homeowners: repair, comfort/airflow, maintenance, replacement shoppers | Mechanical contractors, GCs, property/facility managers, engineers, gyms, retail, churches, restaurants, offices |
| Primary channels | Google Search, GBP, YouTube, Facebook, Nextdoor, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, email, SMS (appropriate use) | LinkedIn, YouTube, direct email, direct business outreach, website, Google, selected Facebook/GBP support |
| Owning pages | Repair & Diagnostics; Airflow & Ductwork; Maintenance; Replacement & Installation | Commercial Service & Maintenance; TAB & Commissioning Support |
| Proof style | Homeowner symptom → measured finding → plain-language explanation | Traverse data, VAV/RTU readings, outside-air verification, deficiency reports, closeout documentation |
| CTA style | "Call / request diagnostics", "Schedule maintenance", "Free estimate" | "Discuss TAB / project scope", "Commercial service discussion" |
| Weeks | 1–8, 12 | 9–11 |

**Do not make every content item serve both audiences.** A residential no-cool video is not a
commercial post with different words — the audience, proof, and CTA are different by design.

## Funnel architecture — six conversion paths

Every campaign follows the master flow:

```
CONTENT / SEARCH
  ↓
RELEVANT OWNING PAGE
  ↓
ONE PRIMARY CTA
  ↓
LEAD RECORD
  ↓
FAST FOLLOW-UP
  ↓
BOOKED SERVICE / ESTIMATE / PROJECT DISCUSSION
  ↓
FIELD EXECUTION
  ↓
FIELD PROOF
  ↓
REVIEW / REFERRAL REQUEST
  ↓
REACTIVATION / REPEAT BUSINESS
```

| Path | Owning page | Route content here when the subject is… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Repair & Diagnostics | `/services/ac-repair-diagnostics/` | no-cool, warm air, not starting, intermittent failure, breaker trips, freeze-ups, electrical/control faults |
| 2. Airflow & Ductwork | `/services/airflow-ductwork/` | hot rooms, weak registers, humidity discomfort, static pressure, return/supply restrictions |
| 3. Maintenance | `/services/ac-maintenance/` | seasonal service, $75 visit scope, maintenance vs repair |
| 4. Replacement & Installation | `/services/replacement-installation/` | repair-vs-replace, ductwork during replacement, quote comparison, installation quality |
| 5. Commercial HVAC | `/services/commercial-service-maintenance/` | RTU/light-commercial service, property-manager needs, commercial maintenance |
| 6. TAB & Commissioning | `/tab-commissioning-support/` | TAB, duct traverse, total airflow, outside air, building pressure, commissioning support |

**Do NOT route every campaign to the homepage.** Examples: hot-room content → Airflow &
Ductwork; no-cool content → Repair & Diagnostics; maintenance content → Maintenance;
quote-comparison content → Replacement & Installation; TAB content → TAB & Commissioning;
commercial content → Commercial Service. The homepage is for brand/direct and the primary
local queries, not for routing service intent.

- **Short clips:** (1) AC runs but does not cool; (2) refrigerant is not automatically the
  problem; (3) why airflow gets checked; (4) legitimate Field Proof from a no-cool call.

  Nextdoor, Shorts.
- **GBP:** symptom + field photo + diagnostic CTA. **Facebook:** job-story format.

- **Field Proof requirement:** one genuine no-cool diagnostic asset (measurement with
  context).
- **Website action:** support the existing repair FAQ and internal linking (no new page).

  path; GBP calls.

## Website integration rules

- **Do not create disconnected blog content merely because a weekly theme exists.** Content
  feeds the owning page.
- Owning pages should include, where appropriate: the recognizable customer problem, service
  explanation, process, symptoms, diagnostic approach, legitimate Field Proof, FAQ, CTA above
  the fold, CTA after major sections, relevant internal links, service-area context, real
  review/testimonial content, and appropriate structured data.
- **Primary internal relationships:** Repair ↔ Maintenance; Repair ↔ Replacement; Airflow &
  Ductwork ↔ Replacement; Commercial ↔ TAB; city/service hubs → appropriate owning service
  page.
- Website changes implied by this plan are **recommendations/backlog items** unless the repo
  already authorizes the specific change. Nothing in this plan authorizes changing pricing,
  warranties, testimonials, service areas, financing, promotions, certifications, job counts,
  or experience claims.

## Field Proof requirement (strategy view)

Every week carries one legitimate Field Proof opportunity (listed per week above). Field Proof
is never staged; measurements always carry context; the full capture SOP, privacy rules and
caption formula live in `docs/marketing/CONTENT-OPERATING-SYSTEM.md`.

## UTM and attribution

Every post or video description that links to the website uses the exact prepared
platform/service link from `docs/marketing/UTM-MASTER-LINKS.md` (owner cheat sheet:
`WHERE-TO-PASTE-UTM-LINKS.md`). No one-off UTM naming; no UTMs in internal navigation,
canonicals, or the sitemap; Google Ads remains auto-tagged and separate. Per-platform link
mapping is in the operating system.
