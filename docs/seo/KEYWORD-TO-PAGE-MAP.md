## Cannibalization rules

1. The homepage never targets `ac repair spring hill` — that belongs to the repair page.
2. `/faq/` answers questions but links to the owning service page; FAQ titles stay
   question-shaped, not commercial.
3. The hub targets *local service* intent; it must not copy service-page bodies. Its unique
   content is local context, communities, and local FAQs.
4. County/city pages beyond Spring Hill do not exist yet (see `LOCAL-PAGE-ROADMAP.md`); until
   then, city modifiers are supported by the hub, service pages, and service-area page.
5. Brand terms are supported by the repair page's brand list — no brand pages.
