# Rank / market monitoring plan — September 2026

## Ground rules

- **Never promise or report a single "rank".** Local results vary by searcher location,
  device, personalization, time, and Google's distance calculation. A local-pack position is
  not a universal number.
- Track **representative markets separately**, from a consistent device/account and, where
  possible, a consistent approximate location.
- Record what is observable (organic presence, local pack presence, GBP visibility) rather
  than absolute positions.

## What to record per query per checkpoint

- Date and checkpoint
- Organic presence: does sunshineclimatesolutions.com appear in the top visible results?
- Local pack: is the business visible in the map pack? (name only; no position claims)
- Which SCS page appears
- Notable competitors above/below (names only)
- GBP signals: rating/review count visible, hours/category correctness

## Method notes

- Use a logged-out browser window; note the approximate location used.
- Do not use rank-tracking services that scrape Google at scale (ToS risk); manual spot checks
  plus GSC/GBP data are sufficient at this business size.
- Review monthly, not daily. Trends over quarters matter more than day-to-day wobble.
