## Cross-competitor synthesis

1. **Title patterns:** `[Service] + [City], FL | [Brand]` dominates; SCS titles now follow the
   pattern with honest differentiators.
2. **City-page architecture:** county→city grids or flat `/service-area/<city>` lists; none
   publish measurement data or report excerpts. SCS deliberately built **one** high-quality
   hub instead of a grid (`LOCAL-PAGE-ROADMAP.md`).
3. **Taxonomy:** residential norm = repair/install/maintenance/ductwork/IAQ/emergency;
   commercial norm = repair/install/maintenance/RTU/controls. **TAB is a separate vendor
   category** — no Spring Hill residential competitor offers it.
4. **Content depth:** genuine technical process content is the clearest whitespace. SCS's
   maintenance page (1164+ words) and diagnostic FAQs already occupy it.
5. **Trust proof:** competitors lean on years-in-business and review counts (unverifiable on
   some sites). SCS cannot compete on invented numbers — it competes on transparency
   ($50/$75 from config), repair-first policy, and measurement-based findings.

## Deliberate non-actions

- No comparison pages naming competitors (risk, low value at this size).
- No imitation of OEM-dealer badge walls (SCS is not a dealer).
- No review-count claims; the reviews collection stays empty until genuine reviews exist.
